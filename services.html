Frequently asked questions

Common Questions

Who determines whether a lead is qualified?

Your firm does. We apply the criteria approved by your team.

How are qualified leads delivered?

By email with a completed intake summary or, where available, through an API integration.

Can you use our current intake questions?

Yes. We can follow your existing script or organize it into a consistent workflow.

Can criteria vary by case type?

Yes. Each case type can have its own questions and standards.

What happens to unqualified inquiries?

The outcome and disqualification reason can be recorded in the partner portal.

Do you make the final decision to accept a case?

No. Your firm retains all final decision-making authority.
