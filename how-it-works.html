How it works

Your Firm Sets the Criteria. We Handle the First-Level Screening.

A clear process from first call to qualified lead delivery.

1

Set the Criteria

We document your accepted case types, intake questions, and qualification standards.

2

Screen the Inquiry

Our team completes the intake and applies your approved criteria.

3

Deliver the Lead

Qualified leads are sent by email with a completed intake summary or through an available API integration.

4

Your Firm Reviews

Your team makes the final decision on whether to pursue or accept the matter.

Common disqualification examples

Criteria Defined by Your Firm

Examples include:

  • The matter falls outside the firm’s practice area.
  • The incident occurred outside the firm’s accepted jurisdiction.
  • The date falls outside the firm’s timing requirements.
  • The caller is already represented.
  • The matter does not meet minimum case criteria.
  • The caller is seeking a service the firm does not provide.
  • Required information cannot be confirmed.
Clear roles. Clear expectations.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not decide whether your firm should accept a case.
  • We do not provide legal advice.
  • We do not replace attorney or staff review.
  • We do not apply criteria your firm has not approved.
  • We do not use one intake process for every firm.
